# The sculpt topology (plan)

Status: workstream 1 (Core) landed on `sculpt/core`, 2026-10-21: the format, the loader, the ModelData transform, `Character` integration, the detail levels on mixed faces, the converter and the tests, with a test-only placeholder patch. Workstream 4 (Downstream) landed on `sculpt/downstream`, 2026-10-21: the VRM export, the base-mesh lookups, the fit on `sculpt`, `opensculptboy convert` and their tests (see "Downstream" below). Workstream 2 (Eye corner) landed on `sculpt/eye-corner`, 2026-10-21: the shipped file holds the eye-corner patch (see "Eye corner" below); the ear-root patch is still to come. Branch `feat/sculpt-topology`.

## Goal

`sculpt` is OpenSculptBoy's own version of anny's mesh. It is anny's mesh with patches where anny's surface cannot follow a detailed character well. The first two patches are the inner corner of the eye and the root of the outer ear. On a real fitted character (a game replica), the fit leaves a dark hole in the inner corner, because anny has a deep pocket of skin there that the replica lacks. It also leaves a crack where the ear meets the cheek, because the game's ear is a separate piece stitched to the head, while anny's ear and head share one coarse skin. Fixes in the fit have reduced both artefacts. A better base mesh removes them for every character.

Every part of anny keeps working on `sculpt`: the phenotypes, local changes, face shapes, facial actions, the rig and its skin weights, the craniofacial landmarks, the detail levels, the texture bake, the glTF and VRM exports and the reference fit. A card made on `anny` loads on `sculpt` with the same parameters, and a converter carries the detail offsets of a card between both meshes.

## Design

### Append-only patches

`sculpt` keeps every vertex of anny at its own index and in its own order. A patch removes some of anny's faces, appends new vertices after anny's last vertex, and adds new faces over the old and new vertices. Code that indexes anny's vertices (the eyeball vertices of the VRM tables, the base-mesh labels, the ear weights, the hair's anatomy, `masks.ear` work in the fit) therefore keeps working unchanged. A vertex of anny that no face uses any more stays in the vertex list as an unreferenced vertex: the exporters write only referenced vertices (the glTF body already writes the vertices of its faces), and the fit and the measures leave them out (`Topology.referenced`).

### New vertices are fixed blends of anny's vertices

Each new vertex is a convex combination of at most 8 anny vertices (its anchors), with non-negative weights that sum to 1: `p_new = sum_k w_k p_anchor_k`. `anny.models.model_transforms.apply_retopology` takes exactly such rows (with `check_weights=True`), so every blend shape, every facial action, every face shape, the skin weights and the landmarks carry over to the new vertices in one step, and they stay exact under every parameter setting. Kept vertices take the identity row. A convex combination can lie off anny's surface, inside the hull of its anchors, so a patch can bridge a pocket (a caruncle spanning the rim of the eye-corner pocket) or add a denser ring of vertices around the ear root.

### Quads and triangles

Patches are written on the quad mesh (`anny-quads`), since the detail levels subdivide it with Catmull-Clark. `sculpt-quads` is the patched quad mesh, and `sculpt` its triangulation, as `anny` is the triangulation of `anny-quads`. New faces are quads where possible; a triangle is allowed where a patch joins anny's faces (Catmull-Clark accepts mixed meshes, and `opensculptboy.detail.subdivision` must keep working on them).

### UVs

A patch keeps anny's texture atlas. Each new face takes its UVs from the same convex combination of its anchors' UVs, within one UV chart: a patch only replaces faces inside one chart and covers the same region of the atlas, so the bake, the head tile, the ear tile and the eye texture box keep their layout. A test checks that no new UV triangle flips and that the new faces cover the removed faces' region without overlap.

### Data file

One file, `src/opensculptboy/data/topology/sculpt.safetensors` with a JSON sidecar `sculpt.json`, holds the composed patches:

- `base`: `"anny-quads"` and a digest of anny's quad faces and template vertices (the build refuses another base);
- `removed_faces`: indices into anny's quad faces;
- `anchors` (M, 8) int64 and `weights` (M, 8) float64: the new vertices (unused slots: anchor 0, weight 0);
- `faces`: the new faces as quads (a triangle repeats its last index), over vertex indices where index N + m is new vertex m;
- `uv_faces` and `uvs`: the new faces' UV indices and the new UVs, appended to anny's UVs;
- `labels`: per new vertex, its patch (`"eye_corner"`, `"ear_root"`), and per new face the same;
- the version and, per patch, the script and options that built it.

Authoring scripts build the file deterministically and left-right symmetrically: one module per patch under `src/opensculptboy/topology/patches/` and a builder `python -m opensculptboy.topology.build` that composes them in a fixed order and writes the file. Patches must not overlap: each claims its faces, and the builder refuses a face claimed twice.

### Code

- `opensculptboy.topology.sculpt`: loads the file, checks the digest, and turns anny's `ModelData` into the patched `ModelData` (removed faces, appended rows through `apply_retopology`, UVs); `sculpt_model(rig, phenotypes, ...)` gives an Anny model on the new mesh.
- `Character.topology` accepts `"sculpt"` (and `"sculpt-quads"` for the detail layer), and `Character.build_model` builds it. A card on `anny` stays byte-identical to today's output.
- `opensculptboy.topology.convert`: `to_anny` and `to_sculpt` for vertex fields of a level (detail offsets, per-vertex maps): identity on anny's own vertices, and on the patched regions a barycentric transfer from the closest points of the other mesh. The change of `src/anny` stays at zero if the integration can live in `opensculptboy`; a hook in `anny` is allowed only where the model itself must change, and kept small for upstream merges.

## API (workstream 1)

The contract of a patch module is in `src/opensculptboy/topology/README.md`: a module of `opensculptboy.topology.patches` with `NAME`, `ORDER` (eye_corner 100, ear_root 200, placeholder 900), `ENABLED`, `OPTIONS` and `build(base: BaseQuadMesh, **options) -> Patch`, authored against `opensculptboy.topology.base_quad_mesh()` (anny's quad mesh at the default body).

- `opensculptboy.topology.patch`: `Patch(name, removed_faces, anchors, weights, faces, uv_faces, uv_anchors, uv_weights, vertex_labels=None, face_labels=None, symmetric=True, options={})` in its own numbering (`N + m` a new vertex, `U + j` a new UV), with `mirrored`, `union` and `with_mirror`; `blend_rows([{anchor: weight}])`; `validate_patch(patch, base)` and `check_symmetry`, raising `PatchError`. New UVs are convex rows over anny's UVs (`uv_anchors`, `uv_weights`), so that the mirror image of a patch carries its UVs over.
- `opensculptboy.topology.sculpt`: `compose(names=None, include=(), exclude=(), base=None, options=None) -> SculptData`; `SculptData.write(path)` and `read_sculpt_data(path)` (checks the sidecar's SHA-256 and the digest of anny's quad mesh); `sculpt_data()` (the innermost `use_sculpt_data(data)` block, else `OPENSCULPTBOY_SCULPT_TOPOLOGY`, else the shipped file); `sculpt_model_data(base_data, data=None, triangulate=False)`; `sculpt_model(rig, topology="sculpt" | "sculpt-quads", local_changes, facial_actions, phenotypes, ..., data=None)`; `build_model(topology, **kwargs)` for any topology.
- `python -m opensculptboy.topology.build [--with P] [--without P] [--only P] [--out FILE]` writes `src/opensculptboy/data/topology/sculpt.safetensors` and `sculpt.json`, deterministically.
- `Character(topology="sculpt")` and `"sculpt-quads"` build through `sculpt_model`; `opensculptboy.detail.subdivision.level("sculpt", n)` subdivides `sculpt-quads`, with `catmull_clark_mixed` on level 0 when it holds triangles (the general rules; quad meshes keep anny's `catmull_clark`), and its cache follows the current sculpt data.
- `opensculptboy.topology.convert`: `to_sculpt(field, subdiv=0, vertices=None, data=None)`, `to_anny(...)`, `LevelCorrespondence(subdiv)` and `convert_offsets(source, target, topology)`.

Choices of the core:

- `src/anny` is unchanged: `sculpt_model` builds anny's `anny-quads` ModelData (cached by anny), patches it and calls `Anny.from_model_data`. Every row goes through `apply_retopology(check_weights=True)` except the blend shapes, whose rows follow its formula outside it (identity rows copied bit for bit, new rows summed as it sums them), which saves several GB with every blend shape. Anny's rows keep their skin weights bit for bit (apply_retopology renormalises them).
- Faces of `sculpt-quads`: anny's quads without the removed ones, in anny's order, then the new faces. `sculpt` triangulates the kept quads as anny does and keeps the triangles.
- The new vertices take `base_mesh_vertex_indices` from 19158 (the vertex count of MakeHuman's `base.obj`) upward, so a lookup of a base-mesh table at a new vertex fails loudly instead of reading another vertex.
- The bone data (heads, orientations, landmarks) does not depend on the vertices and is anny's own; procrustes buffers index anny's vertices, which keep their indices.

Known places that assumed anny's mesh, which workstream 4 handles (see "Downstream"): `export.vrm.check_topology` and `vrm_tables.VRM_TOPOLOGIES` refuse `sculpt` (`TopologyConfig.from_string`); `vrm_tables.skin_weight_digest` changes with the new vertices, so `skin_weight_data` warns and the refinement edits need a sculpt entry; the tables indexed by base-mesh indices (`fit.register.excluded_vertices` and `ear_weights`, `fit.metrics` regions, `posing.head` MediaPipe lookup, `anny.keypoints.KeypointsRegressor`, `anny.viewer.landmarks`, `anny.poses.authoring.rig`) index past their arrays at the new vertices; `fit.register.fit_model` builds `anny.Anny()` and not the card's topology; `export.body.triangulated_faces` now drops the empty half of a `[a, b, c, c]` face, but other code that splits `(F, 4)` faces (`anny.utils.subdivision.quads_to_triangles`, `MixedSubdivision`) assumes quads; `texture.ears` and the ear UV files, `eyes.mesh.eyed_mesh` and its caruncle, the bake and the head and ear tiles take the topology name but were only checked on anny; the eyes' caruncle should drop on `sculpt` once the eye-corner patch lands; the budgets count anny's vertex numbers.

## Downstream (workstream 4)

Every downstream part takes a `sculpt` card. `sculpt_model` records its data on the model (`model.sculpt_data`, and `model.sculpt_bone_slots`, anny's number of skin-weight slots), and `opensculptboy.topology.lookup` reads it:

- `base_mesh_rows(model)`: the MakeHuman base-mesh index of each vertex, a new vertex taking that of its strongest anchor, for tables of labels; `base_mesh_values(model, table, blend=True)`: a numeric table of base-mesh vertices, a new vertex taking the weighted mean of its anchors' rows; `strongest_anchor`, `vertex_anchors`, `patch_labels` and `labelled(model, prefixes)` (the new vertices of a patch label or its finer labels), `referenced_vertices` and `model_topology`. On another topology each is the plain lookup.
- The fit (`opensculptboy.fit.register`): `excluded_vertices`, `lip_margin` and `metrics.anny_regions` read their base-mesh labels through `base_mesh_rows`, so a new vertex takes the label of its strongest anchor, and anny's vertices that no face uses any more are left out as closed parts of their own. `PATCH_ROLES` overrides that for the new vertices of a patch label: `"fit"` (fitted as skin) or `"exclude"` (left out and moved by `follow_skin`, as the eye sockets); it sets `eye_corner`, `eye_corner/caruncle` and `eye_corner/conjunctiva` to `"exclude"`, so that the eye-corner patch's caruncle and fold follow the skin as anny's sockets do (`test.test_sculpt_eye_corner`). `ear_weights` and `pinna` need nothing: the ear-translation targets carry over to the new vertices as blends.
- The ear root line: the ear-root patch labels the new vertices on the line where the outer ear meets the head `"ear_root/line"` (`lookup.EAR_ROOT_LINE_LABEL`), and `ear_root_line(model)` gives their mask (None without them). `pinna(model, rest, ears, height, root=True)` then takes the outer ear as the part of the mesh that the line cuts off the head, the line included, so that the border of the mask, where the ear split attaches the ear to the reference's attachment line, runs along the patch's line; without a line it keeps the height rule. A mask can also be passed as `root`.
- `FitOptions.topology` (`"anny"` or `"sculpt"`; `opensculptboy fit --topology sculpt`) picks the mesh: `fit_model(topology)` builds it through `opensculptboy.topology.build_model`, the card takes its topology, the levels, the measures, the bake (head tile, ear tile, `eye_uv_box`), the residuals and `FitResult.write` follow `character.topology`, and `fit_reference` and `fit_levels` refuse a model of another topology than the options or the card. The ear tile's UV file records the card's topology.
- The VRM export: `check_topology` and `vrm_tables.VRM_TOPOLOGIES` take `sculpt` and `sculpt-quads` (anny's eyeballs keep their base-mesh indices). `skin_weight_digest` covers anny's own rows on a sculpt model (sculpt keeps them bit for bit), so the refinement edits of `data/vrm/skin_weights.safetensors` apply unchanged to anny's vertices, and `vrm_tables.blend_new_rows` gives each new vertex whose anchors' rows the edits or the refinement changed the blend of their new rows (the optimiser is not re-run). The budgets count the file's triangles, so a detail level of `sculpt` steps down to the head region or a coarser level as on `anny`.
- `anny.keypoints.KeypointsRegressor`: vertices past the base mesh take no weight (a two-line change in `src/anny`), so the COCO keypoints regress from anny's vertices as before; the craniofacial landmarks already did. `posing.head.face_mesh_rest` reads anny's vertices, which keep moving with the model even where no face uses them.
- The head tile's caches (`texture.tiles`) follow the current sculpt data. `texture.ears`, `eyes.mesh.eyed_mesh`, the bake and `export.detail.resolve` work on the levels' triangles and needed no change. `anny.utils.subdivision.quads_to_triangles` and `MixedSubdivision` stay quad-only: only the viewer and the corrective authoring use them, on `anny-quads`; the detail layer uses `opensculptboy.detail.subdivision`, which handles mixed faces. `anny.viewer.landmarks` and `anny.poses.authoring.rig` build their own anny models and stay as they are.
- `opensculptboy convert card.json --to sculpt --out card_sculpt.json` (and `--to anny`) writes the card on the other mesh (`topology.convert.convert_card`): the same parameters, each level's detail offsets converted (`convert_offsets`, on the card's own rest body unless `--default-body`) and written next to the new card as `<card>.detail<N>.safetensors`, the ear tile's UVs converted as a field of their level (a UV whose transfer touches a vertex off the ears leaves the ears), and the card's other files named from the new card's folder. The textures stay on anny's atlas.

`test.test_sculpt_downstream` composes the placeholder patch on the back of the head and checks the lookups, the masks and regions (equal to anny's on anny's vertices), `PATCH_ROLES`, `pinna` with a root line, the COCO keypoints, the skin-weight digest and the blended rows, the GLB and VRM files (both versions, the structural checks of `test_vrm_schema`, strict budgets, the refined weights with the data file), a VRM file of a detail level that steps down to the head region, the eyes of `opensculptboy.eyes` at levels 0 and 1, a synthetic reference fit on `sculpt` (levels 0 and 1, under 0.5 mm both ways, the patch on the reference), and `opensculptboy convert` both ways.

## Eye corner (workstream 2)

`patches/eye_corner.py` replaces, in each eye, the 49 quads of anny's pocket in the inner corner (the rings of the lids from the ring just inside the eyeball's sphere out to the lid margins, over 135 to 232 degrees about the gaze, 180 toward the nose) with 49 new quads over 33 new vertices: the same connectivity, so the new faces meet anny's along the band's border. Each new vertex has coordinates (s, t) over the band (harmonic: s from the inner ring to the lid margins, t from the upper lid to the lower); its designed position, in the eye's frame on the default character, follows a profile of s: the caruncle rises from the lid margins to the level of the lids around the corner (the grey closing of a front view), the crest of the plica lies 0.1 eyeball radii outside the sphere and 0.08 nearer the gaze than anny's skin met it, and the fold returns under the sphere to the inner ring; toward both ends of the band the design blends into anny's lid margins. The new vertex is the convex combination of at most 8 nearby skin vertices that best reaches it (non-negative least squares). The new quads keep the UVs of the quads they replace (the inner ring is a seam of the atlas, and blends of the anchors' UVs would fold the layout at the plica), so the patch adds no UV. The faces are labelled `eye_corner/caruncle` (the mound), `eye_corner/conjunctiva` (the plica and the fold) or `eye_corner` (where the design blends into anny's lid margins). The left eye is authored and mirrored.

`test.test_sculpt_eye_corner` holds it: a ray test of the corner from the front and from 35 degrees to either side and 20 degrees up or down (orthographic depth rasters, the eyeball as its fitted sphere): no view reaches anny's skin of the socket behind the new surface, and the dark pocket (what lies 0.06 eyeball radii below the level around it) keeps at most half its area, at rest, with the eyes turned 25 degrees in and out, in a blink, on four phenotype corners and two face-shape samples; the caruncle crosses neither the eyeball nor the lids, the patch crosses neither itself nor anny's skin (in a full blink, no more than anny's own pocket), no face turns over further than anny's own faces of the pocket, and Catmull-Clark levels 1 and 2 crease the visible surface less than anny's corner does. On the default character the pocket shrinks from 52 to 15 mm² summed over the 15 views (7.2 to 3.7 mm² in the worst view), on the elongated corner from 92 to 39, and no view reaches the lining of the socket in any of these states; with anny's own eyeball of 72 vertices in place of its sphere, the lining shows in up to 3.7 mm² of a view before and 1.2 after. Of the 49 quads of an eye, 9 are caruncle, 28 conjunctiva and 12 lid margin. The eyes of `opensculptboy.eyes` drop their caruncle part on `sculpt` and draw the patch's caruncle and conjunctiva with the conjunctiva's material (docs/eyes.md).

Limits: a full blink folds the corner under the closed lids as it folds anny's own pocket (flipped and crossing faces, hidden by the lids); anny's 72-vertex eyeball lies inside its sphere, so without the eyes of `opensculptboy.eyes` a ray can pass between its facets and the fold on some faces (up to 1.2 mm² in one view of a face-shape sample).

## Ear-root patch (workstream 3)

Status, 2026-10-21: `patches/ear_root.py` builds, validates and composes, and `test.test_sculpt_ear_root` guards it, but it is off in the shipped file (`ENABLED = False`; `compose(["ear_root"])` or `build --with ear_root` adds it) until its detail levels are as smooth as anny's around the ear.

- Removed faces, per ear (102, 204 in all): the strip of 32 quads that joins anny's pinna (the rule of `fit.register.pinna` on the default body) to the head, all the way around the ear; one ring of the head's quads around it, with the 6 quads that fill the notches of its border; and one ring of 32 of the pinna's quads inside it. Anny's vertices inside the band keep their indices without faces.
- New band, per ear: 409 vertices and 439 quads, no triangle. The root line (64 vertices, labelled `ear_root/line`, the downstream `EAR_ROOT_LINE_LABEL`) runs along the middle of the strip, smoothed over about two of anny's edges; two rows of 64 on each side at 0.3 and 0.6 of the way to the border; and transition units from each border loop (28 vertices outside, 32 inside) to its outer row, chosen by a dynamic programme (one border edge to one, one to three, or two to four). Each new vertex is a barycentric point of anny's triangulated surface (at most three anchors), relaxed and untangled there, with the UV of the same blend.
- On the default body the vertices lie on anny's surface, and the band's faces lie within 0.055 mm of it on average, 0.24 mm at the 95th percentile and 1.7 mm at most: anny's coarse quads at the root fold by up to 100 degrees (along the head's side of the strip), 70 degrees (along the pinna's border) and 50 to 75 degrees across their diagonals, and the band's chords round those folds. The smallest corner angle is 10.6 degrees and the shortest edge 0.54 mm; the patch is exactly symmetric. Over 25 other bodies (phenotype corners, every face shape at random, the ear's local changes alone and mixed) the 95th percentile stays between 0.13 and 0.35 mm and the maximum below 2.6 mm; where anny's own quads at the root fold over (some face-shape and ear-shape settings), the band's faces fold too, and with `ear-flap-incr` at 1 two of its 1756 triangles fold where anny's do not.
- Catmull-Clark levels: the folds between faces in the band stay below anny's own around the root (level 2: 99th percentile 15.4 against 21.3 degrees, max 30.1 against 34.9), but the band's level 2 lies 0.12 mm (mean) and 0.36 mm (p95) off anny's level 2, and it shows gentle ridges on the head above the helix, behind the ear and below the lobe, where the transitions fan out to the head's coarse border loop. This is what keeps the patch off.
- The synthetic pointed-ear fit (`pointed_ear_target`, with `sculpt/downstream`, levels 0 and 1, anny then sculpt): the ears' reverse error falls from 0.48 / 1.54 / 7.7 mm (mean / p95 / max) to 0.28 / 0.78 / 5.1 mm on level 0 and from 0.15 / 0.46 / 3.9 to 0.09 / 0.27 / 3.0 mm on level 1; triangles over 1 mm off fall from 2.4 % to 1.0 % and from 0.08 % to 0.02 %; the head within 15 mm of the ears from 0.26 / 0.97 to 0.14 / 0.46 mm and from 0.08 / 0.27 to 0.05 / 0.18 mm; but the folds the reference lacks rise from 1.5 % to 2.6 % and from 0.9 % to 2.6 %, and on level 1 the normals turned away from 0.06 % to 0.23 %.

## Workstreams

1. **Core** (`opensculptboy/topology/`): the data format, the loader, the ModelData transform, `Character` integration, `sculpt` and `sculpt-quads`, the detail levels on `sculpt-quads`, a placeholder patch for the tests (a small quad split on the back), and the tests: identity on anny's vertices for every blend shape, facial action and face shape; the new rows exactly reproduced; manifold and watertight where anny is; symmetry; UV checks; the glTF export of a `sculpt` card; byte-identical `anny` exports.
2. **Eye-corner patch** (`patches/eye_corner.py`): replace the faces of the inner-corner pocket of each eye with a caruncle and a closed fold of the conjunctiva, so that the corner reads as closed pink tissue from the front and from up to 35 degrees to either side and 20 degrees up or down, at rest, with the eyes looking left and right and in a blink, on levels 0, 1 and 2. The eyes of `opensculptboy.eyes` drop their own caruncle part on `sculpt` (their socket material keeps its pink, wet look on the patch's faces).
3. **Ear-root patch** (`patches/ear_root.py`): replace the faces along the root of the outer ear (the strip where the ear meets the head, from the top of the helix root past the tragus and the lobe to the back) with a denser, regular band of quads, so that the root can bend smoothly from the head into the ear, and the fit's ear split has a clean line to part along.
4. **Downstream and converter**: the fit (`excluded_vertices`, regions, `ear_weights`, the ear split's `pinna` and attachment on the new vertices), the measures, the bake, the eyes, the VRM export (`check_topology`), the budgets, the converter of detail offsets, and the acceptance run on the real character.

Workstream 1 lands the format and the loader first. Workstreams 2 and 3 author their patches against this file format from the start and test them with a local loader until the core lands. Workstream 4 starts with an audit of every place that assumes anny's mesh, then adapts each one once the core lands.

## Acceptance

- Synthetic targets in CI: the inner corner shows no socket skin through the lids from the views above (a ray test), the ear root has no crack or fold that the target lacks (`metrics`), and every other measure of `test.test_reference_fit` holds.
- The real character (private data, outside CI): refit on `sculpt` with `masks.ear`, rendered with `render_compare.py` in Blender against the replica, with no hole in the inner corner and no crack at the ear root, and the concha and tip gains of the ear split kept.
- No regression on `anny`: the full CI tier passes, and `TestGltfReference` holds unchanged.

## Out of scope for the first version

- The web viewer keeps anny's mesh; showing `sculpt` there is a later step.
- SMPL, SMPL-X and SOMA topologies stay as they are.
